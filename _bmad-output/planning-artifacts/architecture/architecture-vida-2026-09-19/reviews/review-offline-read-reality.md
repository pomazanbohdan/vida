# Reviewer gate — offline-read freshness reality/currentness

Reviewed 2026-09-20: `ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md` AD-16, ADR-0003, REQ-ACL-016, `space-membership-contract.md`, and the OWASP/NIST research report. Verdict: **product rule coherent; production enforcement conditional on OQ-0053**. The approved `< 7 days` / `< 24 hours` / critical online-open profile is consistently stated and correctly distinguished from offline mutation leases and Personal Space. The numbers are VIDA risk choices, not OWASP/NIST mandates.

## High findings

1. **No portable restart-proof elapsed-time primitive is demonstrated.** AD-16 requires old clocks/restarts not to extend the accepted age yet leaves proof/time source to OQ-0053 (`ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md:178,209`; `ADR-0003-offline-revocation.md:121-131`). Android [`elapsedRealtime()`](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/os/SystemClock) is monotonic and includes deep sleep, but starts at boot; ordinary wall time can jump. Apple [`ProcessInfo.systemUptime`](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/foundation/processinfo/systemuptime) measures awake time since restart. Windows [interrupt time](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/sysinfo/interrupt-time) begins at system start; its *unbiased* variant excludes sleep. The W3C [High Resolution Time Level 2](https://www.w3.org/TR/hr-time-2/) explicitly permits resetting the global monotonic clock across browser restarts (Sec. 6). **Disposition:** retain OQ-0053 as a hard production gate; specify per-platform trusted evidence, reboot/browser-reload/sleep handling and fail-closed behavior when elapsed real time cannot be proven. Do not imply a single monotonic-clock call makes an offline 7-day/24-hour deadline tamper-resistant across all targets. A fail-closed restart policy may shorten actual offline availability; surface that trade-off to the user.

2. **Research artifact still labels the now-approved profile unapproved.** `research.md:22,43,47` says concrete periods remain OQ-0051 and the 7-day/24-hour/online profile is “не затверджено”; AD-16, ADR-0003, REQ-ACL-016 and the contract now treat it as adopted (`ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md:174-178`; `ADR-0003-offline-revocation.md:123-133`; `access-control-requirements.md:105-109`; `space-membership-contract.md:57-59`). This is a documentation-currentness discrepancy, not a technical disagreement. **Disposition:** append a dated post-decision note to the research artifact stating that OQ-0051 is resolved by ADR-0003 and OQ-0053 remains open; preserve the research's historical candidate wording if desired.

## Medium findings

3. **Authority freshness semantics must not silently become “federated node online.”** The docs correctly say only the *Space authority policy* can renew freshness, and an old peer head/local activity cannot (`ADR-0003-offline-revocation.md:121`; `space-membership-contract.md:57`). For a peer-authoritative Space or an intermittently reachable authority, remaining peers may sync valid data yet not produce acceptable freshness evidence; after 7 days/24 hours the compliant client must still fail closed. **Disposition:** OQ-0053 should define what constitutes authority-confirmed freshness for each authority profile (including challenge/nonce or equivalent anti-replay), who signs it, and UX when the authority—not merely the network—is unavailable. Do not conflate identity/federation nodes, transport reachability and Space authority.

4. **Already-open content needs deadline/resume gates, not only an “open” check.** ADR-0003 and REQ-ACL-016 say standard/protected *reading* blocks at the boundary across all managed surfaces (`ADR-0003-offline-revocation.md:129`; `access-control-requirements.md:107`). If a document stays open while the app is suspended or the device sleeps through expiry, checking only when opening allows overrun. **Disposition:** require checks on every managed read, foreground/resume, and projection/search/export access; test boundary-crossing with an already-open standard/protected view. Critical *already-open* behavior is explicitly OQ-0053 and should stay unclaimed until decided.

## Source-support and positive checks

- [OWASP MASWE-0024](https://mas.owasp.org/MASWE/MASVS-AUTH/MASWE-0024/) addresses sensitive data after **session termination**; applying that pattern to a received `MemberRemoved` is an explicitly labeled VIDA analogy (`research.md:26`). [OWASP Session Management](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Session_Management_Cheat_Sheet.html) supports risk-based web-session timeouts and warns about client-controlled time references. [MASVS-STORAGE-1](https://mas.owasp.org/MASVS/controls/MASVS-STORAGE-1/) concerns safe storage. None specifies 7 days, 24 hours or critical online-open. `ADR-0003-offline-revocation.md:133` and `research.md:20,26,43` correctly label this boundary.
- `research.md:28` correctly treats NIST AAL reauthentication as session authentication, not Space-membership freshness. No current external-technology version is bound by AD-16.
- The documents correctly limit the guarantee to compliant managed clients and acknowledge that offline devices, already exported data and OS banners cannot be remotely erased (`ADR-0003-offline-revocation.md:111-113`; `ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md:118`).
