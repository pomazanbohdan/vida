# Reviewer gate — full Owner removal, reality/currentness lens

Verdict: **conditional pass as a product rule; not implementation-ready**. The revised documents consistently distinguish immediate client-side blocking *after receipt* from impossible remote erasure of an offline device or previously copied plaintext. No new external technology/version claim is introduced by AD-6. Four precision issues remain.

## High

1. **The acceptance fixture still implies a potentially permitted offline write after revocation.** ADR-0003 rejects any operation not authority-accepted before effective revocation, even if created earlier offline (`ADR-0003-offline-revocation.md:94`). The SpaceMembership contract has the matching execution-time rule (`space-membership-contract.md:47`) but retains “allowed pre-cut offline operation and forbidden post-cut operation” (`:61`). “Pre-cut” may mean local creation time to a builder, creating two compliant-looking implementations. **Fix:** say “authority-accepted before the accepted control sequence” for the allowed case; an unaccepted offline operation submitted after that sequence must be rejected and quarantined regardless of client timestamp. Keep OQ-0031 as the implementation gate for causal ordering.

2. **Governance override is not fully bounded at Platform/Space level.** AD-6 and ADR-0001 exempt Owner removal from app/resource hard deny (`ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md:118`; `ADR-0001-layered-access-control.md:61`), yet ADR-0001 still gives Platform→Space precedence to explicit hard deny (`:56-59`). ADR-0002 lets Admin edit “policies” generally (`ADR-0002-default-role-presets.md:58`). An Admin could plausibly author a Space-level policy that blocks Owner removal, or a Platform policy might do so, despite the “only a current Owner” rule. **Fix:** explicitly define a non-delegable governance authorization namespace or narrowly state which Platform/Space prohibitions can override Owner removal; constrain Admin policy edits so they cannot alter Owner-removal authorization, the ≥1 Owner invariant, or its audit/acceptance checks. The product choice about Platform hard deny should be confirmed rather than silently inferred.

## Medium

3. **“Invalidates … cache” overstates what the client can complete synchronously.** AD-6 requires a compliant client to invalidate local “grants/keys/cache” once it learns removal (`ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md:118`); ADR-0003 more precisely requires immediate access blocking and *initiation* of managed-cache cleanup (`ADR-0003-offline-revocation.md:110`). Cache deletion can crash or be incomplete; old plaintext may remain in OS caches, backups or copied data. **Fix:** in AD-6 promise synchronous denial across managed UI/API paths and key-envelope invalidation; describe cache purge as initiated/best effort with verification/retry, not instantaneous secure erasure. The existing offline/copy caveat should remain.

4. **Future-content protection depends on recipient and key-distribution scope, not merely epoch increment.** The documents require revoking all grants of the removed person and rotating affected scope keys (`ADR-0003-offline-revocation.md:144`; `space-membership-contract.md:40,49`). That is a sound target for *authority-controlled* future delivery, but a current member or independently authorized ServicePrincipal could still export or relay plaintext/key material; old accepted ciphertext may already sit in external caches. **Fix:** word the guarantee as refusal by conforming authorities/clients to grant future access and non-issuance of new epoch keys to the removed subject. Do not imply prevention of collusion, old ciphertext redistribution or secure erasure. Define whether capabilities delegated by the removed Owner to separately identified principals survive; “all Space grants of the person” does not answer it.

## Consistency checks passed

- Offline-read default is now bounded coherently: already-synced content stays locally readable until the client receives effective revocation or another valid local-read prohibition (`ADR-0003-offline-revocation.md:116`). It is *not* a promise that remote removal instantly hides data on a disconnected device.
- `MemberRemoved` plus grant revocation replaces mere Owner-role demotion (`space-membership-contract.md:40`), and Admin `manage_members` no longer grants Owner-removal power (`ADR-0002-default-role-presets.md:58,106`).
- AD-6 makes no claim that Iroh or the federated identity node itself enforces Space membership. Authority serialization remains gated by OQ-0031 (`ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md:192`).
