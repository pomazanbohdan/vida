# Recovery rubric review — 2026-09-29

Verdict: **conditional pass**. AD-37/40 preserve the approved Persona/Device separation, provisional recovery, causal controller history, and backup-coverage boundary. No contradiction with `spec-vida-persona-recovery/SPEC.md` was found. Two cross-cutting seams should be made explicit before calling this update an enforceable implementation spine.

## Findings

1. **High — Provisional Device authority is not a separately enforced state at the mutation boundary.** In `ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md:330`, both trusted enrollment and recovery yield a versioned ControllerState transition “before the new Device can … sign accepted operations”; `:349` prohibits shared acceptance of dual-restore branches, but neither sentence directly requires every verifier/application service to reject an operation signed solely under a provisional recovery grant. `SPEC.md:27,30,41` allows only local provisional work until reconciliation. **Fix:** state the invariant in AD-37 or AD-40: provisional grants can decrypt only recovered locally authorized data and sign locally durable candidate operations, but cannot create `authority.accepted`, new shared grants, key distribution, or remote protected reads until a verified current-controller proof/reconciliation upgrades that grant. Add a negative conformance fixture for a peer receiving a validly signed provisional operation before reconciliation. Exact proof remains OQ-0022/0024; this rule does not decide its wire format.

2. **Medium — New controller-history decision is absent from the capability map and implementation gate.** `ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md:459` maps “Persona recovery and new-Device trust” to AD-37 only; `:376` lists AD-36–39, omitting AD-40. Thus two teams can implement enrollment/recovery against AD-37 and miss append-only causal reconciliation, compatible grant union, or contested-Device suspension. **Fix:** include AD-40 in the capability row and explicitly make its positive/negative controller fixtures a pre-fan-out gate: compatible independent grants; conflicting grant/revoke; contested existing Device read/write/key/accepted-op denial; dual recovery provisional; signed reconciliation covering both branches; replayed invitation rejection. Production proof remains OQ-0022/0024.

3. **Medium — Verified bytes and off-Device recoverability need separate names.** `ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md:331` allows a new “backup” to supersede the last known-good after destination readback/authenticated open/coverage validation; it does not say that readback on the same soon-to-be-lost Device cannot prove survival after Device loss. `SPEC.md:29,35-36,50-52` and `recovery-bundle-contract.md:15` separate local save, verified copy, coverage frontier, and available authorized replica. **Fix:** retain the last known-good *verified copy* until replacement verification; claim “recoverable after Device loss” only when an independently reachable encrypted resource copy and the separately retained secret/bundle cover that frontier. Preserve a weaker “export created” label for non-reopenable destinations. Storage/atomic proof detail stays OQ-0024.
