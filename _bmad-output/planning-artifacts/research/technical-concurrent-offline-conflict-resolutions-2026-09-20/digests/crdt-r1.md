# CRDT reference digest — 2026-09-20

- Claim: Automerge concurrent writes to one key retain alternatives through `getConflicts`, while normal read exposes a deterministic visible winner. Source: [Automerge Conflicts](https://automerge.org/docs/reference/documents/conflicts/), Automerge contributors, publication date n.d., accessed 2026-09-20; confidence high for documented behavior.
- Claim: An Automerge assignment made after seeing a conflict clears the exposed conflict. Source: same official page; confidence high. A VIDA business resolution cannot be inferred merely from that library assignment.
- Inference: two disconnected resolution assignments to the same key should become concurrent writes and thus a new library conflict on reunion. The official page does not directly provide this exact two-resolver scenario; confidence medium pending fixture.
- Claim: Loro Map uses Lamport LWW with peer-ID tie-break for concurrent map-key updates. Source: [Loro Map](https://www.loro.dev/docs/tutorial/map), Loro, publication date n.d., accessed 2026-09-20; confidence high for documented behavior. Its ordinary visible value does not implement VIDA's no-winner projection.
- Claim: Loro sync converges for the same update set independent of arrival order; causal DAG/frontiers are available for application-level semantics. Sources: [Loro Sync](https://www.loro.dev/docs/tutorial/sync), [Loro Versioning Deep Dive](https://www.loro.dev/docs/advanced/version_deep_dive), Loro, publication date n.d., accessed 2026-09-20; confidence medium until exact APIs/version are prototyped.
- Hypothesis for VIDA, not a source fact: two causally incomparable, valid, different resolution operations should form a new explicit conflict; semantically identical resolutions may collapse in projection while both provenance operations remain. Requires product approval and conformance fixtures.
- Claim: CRDT merge alone does not provide authorization, transactions or coordination for exclusive-resource decisions. Source: [Loro When Not to Use CRDTs](https://www.loro.dev/docs/concepts/when_not_crdt), Loro, n.d., accessed 2026-09-20; confidence high as official guidance. Do not transfer status/file rule to booking or other hard invariants without resource authority.
- Claim: For opaque registers, concurrent writes may remain for application/user resolution or be arbitrated by timestamps, and timestamp arbitration can drop an update. Source: [Eventually Consistent Register Revisited](https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.05010), Zawirski et al., 2015-11-16, accessed 2026-09-20; confidence high as original research abstract, though older than technical-pattern freshness window and not proof of a specific current library.
