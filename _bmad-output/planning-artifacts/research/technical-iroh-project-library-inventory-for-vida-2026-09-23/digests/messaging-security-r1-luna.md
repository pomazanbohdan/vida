## Synthesis

- Prototype first: (1) OpenMLS membership/removal/replay; (2) Beekem-style encrypted namespace/key rotation plus chunk integrity; (3) Proscenium offline delivery and control/media-plane separation; (4) Kith per-space isolation and device onboarding.
- Keep all four as falsifiable gates: membership removal must prevent old-member access to future writes; offline delivery must survive duplicate/reordered/partial acknowledgements; media control must not expose plaintext signaling or grant media access after revocation; workspace tool access must remain scoped under adversarial inputs.
- No production library choice: repository age, custom crypto, unfinished behavior, ownership discrepancy, and absent audits remain material uncertainties. No standalone adoption recommendation follows from these samples.
