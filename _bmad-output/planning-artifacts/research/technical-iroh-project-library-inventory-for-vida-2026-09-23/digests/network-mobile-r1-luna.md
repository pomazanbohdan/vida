## Prototype gates served

- Iroh endpoint startup/shutdown, persistent identity, and reconnect/failover: `flexaccess-iroh`, Quix, Acerola, and FFI expose complementary patterns; compare rather than transplant.
- NAT/relay behavior: FlexAccess has explicit per-relay probes, relay-only E2E, and in-place home-relay failover. Quix/Acerola exercise application-level peer mesh; neither makes a production guarantee.
- VPN/TUN: Quix only in this set. Flextunnel iOS is intentionally SOCKS/port-forward proxy and has no TUN.
- Mobile lifecycle: flextunnel-ios documents iOS suspension/background keep-alive tradeoffs. 1337farm demonstrates Android `dataSync` foreground transfer service, with restart semantics and bounded operation.
- Cross-language bindings: n0 `iroh-ffi` is the broadest official binding reference; note its documented protocol scope excludes `iroh-blobs`.
- Verified blob transfer: Acerola’s optional `iroh-blobs` adapter and 1337farm’s explicit add/get/fetch/model APIs are prototype evidence. No inspected FFI binding gives a blob API; hash/ticket transfer paths still need integrity/error/cancellation gates.

## Evidence gaps

- No device-level verification performed; background behavior and NAT success claims rely on upstream implementation/docs/tests.
- Quix and Acerola manifest constraints were found, but exact lock-resolved iroh versions were not independently extracted.
- License terms were not verified for `flexaccess-iroh`, `flextunnel-ios`, or `iroh-android-native`; treat as unavailable, not permissive by default.
- The accessible evidence did not expose current commit SHA/date for `flextunnel-ios` or `iroh-ffi`; no version/date is inferred from crawl freshness.
