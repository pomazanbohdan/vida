## Interpretation / limits

Raw volume is not an ordinal maturity score. Rayfish has the largest feature surface and visible Android fixes, but has a sizeable live issue/PR queue (73/8) and no Android TUN parity proof. RDS has dense feature development and rapidly merged PRs, but only a two-day public history at snapshot and unstable wire/API; zero reported issues does not validate quality. Nexa has prototype-worthy Android VpnService structure but only initial commits. iroh-ffi has broader binding/package maturity and historical closed throughput but no default-branch commit during the last 30 days. No sampled PR closure time measures a reviewer’s first response, and no closed-issue aging distribution, severity weighting, maintainer response SLA, or device trial was performed. Prototype gates remain: pinned lock-resolved tuple/build matrix, crash and reconnect behavior, relay-only/symmetric-NAT cases, verified/cancelled/resumed blobs, and physical Android/iOS lifecycle trials.
