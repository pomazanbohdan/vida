# Research brief: Vida identity modes

## Decision served

Define a coherent identity architecture for Vida that supports:

- fully autonomous anonymous operation without registration;
- identity hosted by a selected federated node;
- public, discoverable identity;
- multiple devices, recovery, migration and device revocation;
- deliberate transitions between modes without forced correlation.

## Research dimensions

1. Stable identity, aliases, hosting and public discoverability.
2. Autonomous and anonymous identities, pairwise/contextual personas and invitations.
3. Federated-node accounts, portability and migration.
4. Device trust, recovery, key rotation and compromise handling.
5. Recommended Vida model and unresolved product decisions.

## Evidence policy

Primary specifications and official documentation only. Local project files shape the questions but do not count as external verification.
