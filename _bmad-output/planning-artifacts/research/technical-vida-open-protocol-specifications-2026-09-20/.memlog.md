- (claim by assistant) ref=[2] status=verified class=primary pub=2026-09 — NIP-01 defines signed JSON event and event kind is distinct from document number.
- (claim by assistant) ref=[3] status=verified class=primary pub=2026-09 — Matrix MSC process requires implementation evidence and separates proposals from canonical spec.
- (claim by assistant) ref=[4] status=verified class=primary pub=2026-09 — AT Lexicon describes records, RPC, and streams and defines compatibility constraints.
- (claim by assistant) ref=[5] status=verified class=primary pub=2019-06 — RFC 8610 CDDL expresses CBOR or JSON message structures but not VIDA access control semantics.
- (decision by user) ADOPTED: both independently implementable clients/nodes and third-party Apps/plugins; full transparency of normative contracts and conformance. ADR-0013 and REQ-OPEN-001–006 materialize scope. Exact format, license and governance remain OQ-0055; no Nostr adoption implied.
