# Research brief

Compare Nostr NIPs, Matrix MSCs, AT Protocol Lexicon and formal description languages against accepted VIDA architecture. Distinguish document governance, machine-readable schemas, wire representation, security semantics and independent conformance. Recommend a decision path, not a new adopted protocol.
