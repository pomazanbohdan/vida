- (decision) Product owner approved enabling Tor in an existing Space, transparent application of received policy by other Devices, and ordering two offline Persona toggles by actual action time rather than arrival or sync time.
- (claim) R1-R8 are primary reference observations verified 2026-09-28; see digests/transition-references.md.
- (claim) R9 supports the feasibility limit: millisecond timestamps without bounded clock uncertainty cannot establish exact real-time order across offline Devices.
- (synthesis) Proposed policy-only control-before-data handshake, new control epoch, separate Tor-only endpoint, and fail-closed pending state; not implementation proof or product approval of exact protocol bytes.
- (synthesis) Two Devices isolated before policy delivery can still communicate by their old rules; protocol cannot retroactively prevent that metadata exposure.
