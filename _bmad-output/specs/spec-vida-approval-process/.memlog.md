- (capability) CAP-2: requester submits a durable candidate and receives a separate authority outcome; success is pending after local save/transport sync and accepted/rejected/expired only after signed outcome for matching RequestId, revision and frontier.
- (capability) CAP-3: process can require one authorized approver, ordered stages or threshold M-of-N including all-of M=N; success is Core tally of eligible unique votes and no premature outcome.
- (capability) CAP-4: approvals bind exact protected revision/frontier and current actor rights; success is duplicate/stale/unauthorized votes ignored and protected-data edit starts new revision requiring fresh approvals.
- (capability) CAP-5: approval policy cannot weaken Core governance or exclusive-claim serialization; success is rejected attempt to replace Owner/revocation rules or confirm two incompatible exclusive claims via auto-approve.
- (constraint) AppPackage default is optimal recommendation, not immutable minimum; Admin config is per named process/operation family and limited to Core-supported modes, not arbitrary resolver implementation.
- (constraint) Auto-approve is no manual approver, but still validates signature, rights, preconditions, semantic class and authority; it does not bypass exclusive scarcity or transport/domain evidence separation.
- (constraint) Votes append-only and scoped to Persona, process, RequestId, revision/frontier; several Devices of same Persona cannot produce multiple logical votes.
- (constraint) OWASP Authorization deny-by-default and every-request permission checks are review references; they do not choose peer authority topology or count devices as approvers.
