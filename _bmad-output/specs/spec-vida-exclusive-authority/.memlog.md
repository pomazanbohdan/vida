- (direction) Headless express: distill adopted ADR-0016 and operation-finality semantics plus OQ-0033 research options into a decision-gated BMad spec; do not choose a serverless quorum or authority topology.
- (capability) CAP-1: Equal authorized Devices can durably create and exchange an exclusive-operation candidate; success: same OperationId is recovered after retry/crash and remains pending absent named authority outcome.
- (capability) CAP-2: Resource Authority can accept or reject an exclusive request with independently verifiable bounded outcome; success: all peers given same accepted control and outcome proof converge on RequestId, revision and frontier regardless of packet order.
- (capability) CAP-3: A user can see whether an exclusive request is pending or confirmed during partitions and reconnect; success: no disconnected side displays confirmed from local write, transport ACK, clock or mere replication receipt.
- (capability) CAP-4: Authority membership and grants can change without reviving stale claims; success: revocation and epoch changes prevent a stale actor or minority certificate from accepting a previously pending request.
- (constraint) ADR-0016: no favored physical Device, master, endpoint or replica-ID winner; equal replicas converge only given same valid data and verified control/acceptance state.
- (constraint) Operation finality: exclusive claim/approval remains domain.pending until named Resource Authority signs accepted, rejected or expired outcome for exact RequestId, revision and frontier; serverless without reachable serialized logical authority stays pending.
- (constraint) Replication receipt, recipient delivery, mailbox ciphertext and Iroh packet ACK are separate from domain authority; Iroh relay transports packets and is not Resource Authority.
- (constraint) Per-operation authorization, grant/control frontier, schema/policy and causal prerequisites must be revalidated at acceptance; an old offline candidate or clock cannot restore revoked rights.
- (constraint) Status/file multi-value conflict and mergeable resource rules remain separate OQ-0034; an automatic CRDT winner is not an exclusive business confirmation.
- (question) For each exclusive operation family, who holds logical acceptance authority: Personal Persona, shared Space process, or external resource owner?
- (question) For a shared serverless Space, should an unreachable authority leave the operation pending until reconnection, or should authorized replicas form an intersecting quorum that can confirm on one partition side?
- (question) If quorum is chosen, what membership/rotation/epoch, fault model, revocation and signed certificate bind the acceptance frontier?
- (question) How should a person see a pending booking/claim while offline and after reconnect, distinct from Saved locally and Synchronized?
